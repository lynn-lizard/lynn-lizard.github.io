## Required/Suggested Jobs

### Hard Required
- 2x Thieves  (finding traps and hidden chests/portals)
- 1x Ranger (popping traps since traps pierce invulns)
- 1 Bard for stunning slimes "The First Song" cast in the middle/top platform during Storm Nodes.
  - You can do this with normal stuns but like... no? Just use Romeo's.
- 2+ Dancers for Mes on raidwides. No I'm not joking. Elementry Chemistry does ~700k unmitted. Also its nice for a lot of raidwides.
### Optional Nice Things
- A dispeller on each side of Halls 1 (Necromacner/Time Mage) for Barmuus that do very high damage on death.
  - This is optional but you will need to heavy mit and slowly burn one at a time.
- Black Mage for insane crowd control (and damage) w/ Toad during trash
- Knights for comfort, that 10% mit is real nice for some fights.
- Blue Mage for Mighty Guard on some raidwides.

A1-B2-C3 for party platform splits

Nodes will have interruptible cast every so often called "Thunderclap Concerto"
**Nodes have to die at the same time else you wipe to an enrage**

Top platform has to call cyclops for the bottom platforms to dodge (they spawn NS or EW, we have to go where they aren't)
- Top also has black slimes that cast "The First Song" that must be stunned or they kill you.
  - Use Phantom Bard's Romeo's Ballad to stun these easily.

Bottom platforms dodge cyclops AoEs based on call from up top. These are done *per platform, please pay attention. it could be best to use yell chat for this. Ex. NS EW or NS NS (left plat - right plat)*
After dodges a set of red slimes appear, they just hurt a lot. Tank and spank on top of the node.
#### Timeline
- Thunderclap Concerto
  - Interrupt!!
- Cyclops 1
  - Top groups calls NS or EW safe for each side individually.
  - Bottom group dodges out at "Five seconds until fatal Onslaught"
- Thunderclap Concerto
  - Interrupt!!
- Slimes 1
  - Tanks grab mobs
  - Top stuns with Romeo's Ballad (Arcane Stop), bottom tanks/spanks. Slimes hurt.
- Cyclops 2 *(suggest holding bosses till AFTER this resolves)*
  - Top group calls safe spots for both sides
  - Thunderclap Concerto Interrupts again before heading out!!
- Slimes 2
  - Should die here, we saw slimes appear and instantly vanish each time.

## Miniboss: Clockwards
#### Curseclock adds are weak to Lightning
Between Necrophobia and this encounter, you'll be able to spawn the support mob and maybe get an extra life if you have enough people outside assuming you did the puzzle earlier.
- <a href="https://raidplan.io/plan/wwvzbkbga2js88ya#25" traget="_blank">Raidplan</a>

The short version is that you have two clocks, one on the wall and another on the ground. You must make it so the ground clock matches the wall clock by killing the **Tower Curseclock** adds. Each add, when killed, adjusts the clock the following amounts:
- Large = 3 hours
- Medium = 1 hour
- Small = 15 minutes
- Tiny = 5 minutes.

These Curseclock adds, in varying sizes, will appear at the very edge of the room and do *nothing until interacted with*. You should assign a tank or two to grab the adds in a very controlled manner. Just single target these adds down individually until you match the appropriate time.
#### PLEASE WATCH ALL LARGE AOE SKILLS (Assize, Dances, Stars, etc...)! DO NOT RISK PULLING EXTRA MOBS FOR NO REASON!!
There is also a **Lockward** that spawns, just tank it and leave it alive (ignore it too, its just absurdly bulky). It will do the occasional raidwide but it is not lethal.
#### Example
The clock shows `7:40`. We must kill
- 2 Large Curseclocks (+6 hours)
- 1 Medium Curseclock (+1 hour)
- 2 Small Curseclocks (+30 minutes)
- 2 Tiny Curseclocks (+10 minutes)
This will adjust the clock to say 7:40 and instantly end the encounter despawning all mobs.

*__If you take ~3 minutes(?) the tower will kick you out to the Pavillion back in North Horn.__*

## Forked Tower: Magic (Extreme) Rewards
- Twin Snakes: 1 amulet
- Halls 1: 1 amulet
  - Once you clear you can return back and visit the other side for the other chest and +1 amulet.
- Sword Dancer: 2 amulets
- Halls 2: 1 amulet
- Storm Nodes: 1 amulet
- Necrophobe: 2 amulets
- Puzzle Completion Chest: 3 amulets
  - Spawns after Boss 3 if puzzle is done correctly.
- Clockwards: 1 amulet
- Final Halls: 2 + 2 amulets
  - 2 chests to claim
- Index: 5 amulets
  - 3 extra amulets if the Hermetic Recluse (support mob) dies before killing Index
#### Total: 25 amulets.

### Extra Stuff
- Each boss gives a field note
- Hermetic Recluse (Support mob), Necrophobe, and Index have an uncommon chance for a Duck-billed Porter Whistle (Lockwards mount)
- Chests have a random chance to reward a Final Final Fixative
