This is the second zone of the Occult Crescent released in patch 7.55; it contains many FATEs, Critical Encounter,
and the Forked Tower: Magic (Normal and Extreme). This zone also serves as setting for the last  half of the story. 

<br/><hr/>

## Unlocking North Horn
To unlock the North Horn you must have completed the South Horn story (finishing the <a href="https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Mysteries_Abide" target="_blank">"Mysteries Abide"</a> quest). 
To continue the story and unlock North Horn you must begin the quest <a href="https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Occult_Reunion" target="_blank">"Occult Reunion"</a> in Tuliyollal at the Expedition Messanger.

Once progressed enough, you will gain the ability to enter the North Horn and explore onwards.

:::caution[Knowledge Level Requirement?]
There is no knowledge level requirement to enter North Horn! Just finish the story and continue on!

However the zone has mobs that are all 20+, so traversal might be diffcult at times. But the mobs are spaced out enough so it would cause many problems (hopefully).
:::

Auroral Mirages will not despawn weather specific mobs. The zone’s actual weather cycle will remain active in the background. A similar behavior is found with Umbral Turbulence (Ovni weather) in Eureka: Hydatos.

Killing these mobs rewards roughly double or triple the gold for their level,and these rewards are affected by chaining; so, 
a level 48 weather mob can reward up to 48 gold! Most of these mobs are stationary, though Crescent Fools do wander a large set path through the zone.

### Ashkin/Undead
Undead mobs (referred to as Ashkin by the game) spawn between **10pm and 4am** Eorzean time. All undead are proximity and blood (low hp) based aggro.

:::note[Undead Mobs Give More Gold!]
When being killed, undead mobs will grant more gold similar to weather mobs.
:::

## Critical Encounters
Critical Encounters, or CEs from here on, are the beefed up version of FATEs. 
These battles are against fiercer foes and can sometimes reward new Phantom Jobs, Field Notes, as well as more experience and silver. 
CEs are split into two kinds: ones that can be spawned by the player, and ones that spawn randomly without player input. 
The rewards are the same regardless of spawn method.

Every successful Critical Encounter will reward the following
- 400-260,420 Knowledge EXP (scales to your knowledge level)
- 720 Phantom Job EXP
- 20 uncapped tomestones
- 10 weekly capped tomestones
- 200 enlightenment silver obol pieces
- 10 of a Phantom Dispeller (drops are CE dependant, see Phantom Weapons section)
- Field Notes (specified below)
- Rarely an occult accessory

### Tracking Critical Encounters
You can use Infi’s Occult Tracker website to help you keep track of which FATEs and CEs have spawned. 
This site will also help you know which encounters drop which  rewards (Field Notes, Demiatmas, etc.) 
along with which CEs can be spawned by players and which mobs will spawn them.

- <a href="https://tracker.xivstats.com/" target="_blank">Infi's Tracker</a>
- <a href="https://ffxiv-occult.com/" target="_blank">Occult Tracker</a>

## Forked Tower Magic: Normal
:::danger[Normal Mode is like Castrum Lacus Litore or Dalriada]
The Normal mode tower is meant to be cleared and enjoyed by everyone of any skill level. It is no where near the difficulty that Forked Tower: Blood had.

Please do not fret and do enjoy the content! Its quite nice.
:::

Once unlocked you can enter the Forked Tower: Magic in the South West shore of the central lake. There's a pavillion that will glow during **Auroral Mirages** and take anyone standing
on the Pavillion inside after a recruitment period. Once inside you will be tasked with defeating bosses and travesing the the tower. 

**There are no enrages, lives, hidden traps, extra secret encounters, or anything that could cause a total wipe of the group.**

You can earn Arcane Amulets by defeating bosses and finding hidden chests using Thief's percept/unlock skills.

### Rewards
- Up to 14 arcane amulets
- 800 silver
- 2880 phantom job exp (swap to a leveling job before the final boss!)
- Spawns 1 silver (with a 10% chance for a second) and 2 bronze chests

### How to Spawn Forked Tower: Magic
The weather will change to Auroral Mirages, meaning the the tower is open for recruiting, under the following conditions:
- One person in the instance is knowledge level 40 with Forked Tower: Magic unlocked.
- A hidden timer of 60 minutes has passed
  - This timer can be reduce by 5 minutes for every CE completed and 1 minute for every FATE completed.

## Currencies
There are currently 3 different types of currency that are all earned in (mostly) unique ways and can be exchanged for mounts, 
glamours, gear progression, ciphers for Forked Tower: Magic, or other resources to aid you in taking on the South Horn. 
Silver obols are from FATEs, CEs, and Pots/Bunnys, gold obols are earned from chaining mobs or Pots/Bunnys, and 
Arcanne Amulets are from Forked Tower: Magic (similar to Eurekan Fragments from Baldesian).

## Gold Obol Farming
Gold farming is primarily done with the use of Phantom Thief tanks, Thief sages, and jobs such as Cannoneers, pSummoners, Necromancer, or pBlack Mages to kill groups of high
level mobs before they overwhelm the group. Most groups tend to use the far-side of the Dark Territory located in the
Subterrane section of the map. They go in circles gathering and killing the Jesters and Bombadeels until satisfied.

More efficient solo farming routes are still being worked out! However have a preliminary guide made by PerryTheFridge aka Spiral Lance:
